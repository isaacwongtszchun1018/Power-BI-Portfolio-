# Monthly Traffic Flow Aanlysis: Before and After Tai Lam Tunnel New Toll Implementation on 31st May 2025 Dashboard (Power BI)

## Dashboard Purpose
Analysing monthly traffic flow changes of Tai Lam Tunnel under the old and new toll plans

### Toll Plan Implementation
•	New toll plan: Implemented on 31 May 2025

## Data Coverage 
•	Start point (November 2024): Last toll adjustment before the franchise ended and operation was handed over to the government.
•	End point (June 2026): A year after the implementation of new toll plan.

## Methodology
Since only monthly traffic data is available from data.gov.hk, the dashboard uses average monthly flows to represent each toll plan:  
•	Old Toll Plan: Average monthly traffic flow between November 2024 – May 2025    
•	New Toll Plan: Average monthly traffic flow between May 2025 – May 2026  

## Data Source
Dataset Title:  
•	Select Item Section 3 : Tunnel, Lantau Link, Bridge and Vehicular Ferry Services Statistics - Table 3.2 (g) - Tai Lam Tunnel(English)  

Data Source: data.gov.hk (Hong Kong SAR Government Open Data Portal)
