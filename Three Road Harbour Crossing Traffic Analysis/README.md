# Monthly Traffic Flow of Three Road Harbour Crossings (Western Harbour Crossing("WHC"), Cross Harbour Tunnel ("CHT"), Eastern Harbour Crossing ("EHC") under old, 633 and 644 Toll Plans Dashboard (Power BI)

## Dashboard Purpose
Analysing monthly traffic flow changes under the Old Toll Plan (prior to the 633 implementation), the 633 Fixed Toll Plan, and the 644 Time-Varying Toll Plan across Hong Kong’s three road harbour crossings.

### Toll Plan Implementation
•	633 Fixed Toll Plan: Implemented on 2 Aug 2023  
•	644 Time-Varying Toll Plan: Implemented on 17 Dec 2023

## Data Coverage 
•	Start point (June 2019): last (concessionary) toll adjustment of the Western Harbour Crossing before the implementation 633 fixed toll plan.  
•	End point (June 2026): latest monthly dataset available from data.gov.hk.

## Methodology
Since only monthly traffic data is available from data.gov.hk, the dashboard uses average monthly flows to represent each toll plan:  
•	Old Toll Plan: Average monthly traffic flow between Jun 2019 – Aug 2023    
•	633 Toll Plan: Average monthly traffic flow between Aug 2023 – Dec 2023  
•	644 Toll Plan: Average monthly traffic flow between Jan 2024 – Jun 2026

## Page 
### Page 1 – Overview
o	Provides a high level comparison of traffic flow across the three road harbour crossings (WHC, CHT, EHC) under the Old Toll Plan, 633 Toll Plan, and 644 Toll Plan. 
### Page 2 – Western Harbour Crossing (WHC)
o	A deep dive analysis of WHC traffic changes under each toll plan, including breakdowns by vehicle class, direction, and monthly trends. 
### Page 3 – Cross Harbour Tunnel (CHT) 
o	A deep dive analysis of CHT traffic changes under each toll plan, including breakdowns by vehicle class, direction, and monthly trends. 
### Page 4 – Eastern Harbour Crossing (EHC) 
o	A deep dive analysis of EHC traffic changes under each toll plan, including breakdowns by vehicle class, direction, and monthly trends. 
### Page 5 – Total Harbour Crossings 
o	Aggregates traffic data from WHC, CHT, and EHC to present a consolidated view of overall harbour crossing traffic changes under each toll plan.
